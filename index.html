4. Deployment pipeline

The deployment path mirrors the project architecture from traffic ingestion through analyst feedback and retraining.
01CollectNetwork connection records enter the IDS.
02PreprocessMissing values, categorical encoding, scaling and feature selection.
03InferenceModel produces a probability-based risk score.
04AlertThreshold converts risk into CLEAR or ALERT.
05Analyst feedbackConfirmed attacks and false positives become labels.
06RetrainValidated new data can be used to update the model.
07HealthWaiting for backend.
08APIDemo / no backend connected.
